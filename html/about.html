About the project

About Us

LaymyoChin Web is a responsive, local-first website built from the LaymyoChin Android app. It keeps the original learning modules, community surfaces, and support links, then presents them as a cleaner web experience.

The dictionary

Words in LaymyoChin, Burmese, and English.

Every entry pairs a LaymyoChin (Sawlai) headword with its Burmese and English meanings, including part-of-speech tags such as (n), (v), and (adj). The dataset covers daily life, people, study, work, and more.
